# Forecast interpretation

## Use forecast evidence carefully

The forecast is a planning estimate for aggregate monthly export volume. Its test MAE, RMSE, and MAPE describe performance on the held-out test period; they do not guarantee future error bounds. Recursive forecasts can drift because each predicted value feeds the next month.

## Explainable evidence

Compare the forecast-year total with the latest observed 12-month total, list forecast peak and low months, and show historical average volume by calendar month. These are descriptive comparisons. They do not establish why demand changes or prove that seasonality causes export volume changes.
