# Export planning heuristics

## Staged commitments

Use point forecasts alongside observed orders, production capacity, inventory, and buyer commitments. Staged contracts and periodic reviews reduce the risk of treating a single forecast path as a guaranteed shipment plan.

## Seasonal capacity planning

Use month-by-month forecast and historical seasonality to schedule internal capacity reviews. Validate the plan against current supply, quality, logistics, and buyer information. Historical monthly peaks alone do not explain demand or guarantee a repeat.

## Destination and product decisions

Destination-specific pricing, product selection, and market-entry decisions require country-level buyer and export evidence plus current destination requirements. Aggregate volume forecasts are insufficient for those decisions.
