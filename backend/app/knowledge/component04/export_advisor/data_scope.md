# Export demand data scope

## What the evidence contains

The monthly export history is an aggregate Sri Lankan tea export series from 2016 through 2025. It includes total export volume in kilograms, export values, weighted FOB prices, and monthly counts of represented countries, processes, and package types. The LSTM target is monthly total export volume in kilograms. The forecast is recursive, so a forecasted month becomes an input to later forecast months.

## What it does not contain

The advisor's forecast workbook does not contain the names or quantities for each destination, tea process, or package. It cannot establish which country or product is growing, and it cannot support country-specific market-share claims. Ask for destination-level export rows before making those claims.
